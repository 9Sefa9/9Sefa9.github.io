About Me

About Me

My name is Sefa Gövercin. I hold a Bachelor of Science in Applied Computer Science from the University of Duisburg-Essen. During my studies I focused on algorithms, data processing and modern software architecture.

My bachelor thesis explored the structural analysis of scientific documents, developing a framework to classify research PDFs based on layout patterns from IEEE papers.

As a tutor in distributed systems, I strengthened my ability to explain complex technical models in clear, practical ways — a skill that supports my consulting work today.

In 2015 I led a development team building high-performance Android games using Unity3D and C#. In 2021 I joined IT.NRW, contributing to large-scale digitalisation projects in the public sector.

Since 2022 I have been working as a Software Engineer & IT Consultant at CGI, focusing on enterprise identity management, cloud migration and secure, scalable software architecture.
